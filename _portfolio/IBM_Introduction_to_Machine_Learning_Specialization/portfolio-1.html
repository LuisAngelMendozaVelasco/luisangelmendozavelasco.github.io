Understand the dataset¶

The Human Activity Recognition Using Smartphones database was created from recordings of study participants performing activities of daily living (ADLs) while carrying a smartphone with built-in inertial sensors. The objective is to classify activities into one of the six activities performed (walking, walking upstairs, walking downstairs, sitting, standing, and laying).

For each record in the dataset the following is provided:

  • Triaxial acceleration from the accelerometer (total acceleration) and the estimated body acceleration.
  • Triaxial angular velocity from the gyroscope.
  • A 561-feature vector with time and frequency domain variables.
  • Its activity label.
